Privacy policy

Familienplaner is a private app used by the members of a single family. This page describes how it handles data, including data from Google accounts.

Who uses it

Only the family members who set up the app. There are no other users and no public sign-up.

Data the app stores

Tasks, shopping list, calendar entries, pickup times and holidays, and the names and e-mail addresses needed to sign in. This data is stored in a private database (Supabase) that only signed-in family members can access.

Google user data

If a family member connects their Gmail account, the app requests read-only access (gmail.readonly). It only reads messages that match a list of senders and keywords that the family maintains. From these messages the app creates suggestions for calendar entries and tasks.

  • Only the suggestion and a link back to the message are stored, not the full message text.
  • Message content is sent to an AI service (Anthropic) solely to produce these suggestions.
  • The app never sends, deletes or changes e-mails.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • No person reads the messages except the account holder.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

A family member can revoke the app's access at any time in their Google account settings under security and third-party access. Stored suggestions are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support address listed on the app's Google consent screen.
